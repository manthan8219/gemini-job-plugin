# UK & Ireland Curriculum Vitae (CV) Guidelines

## Legal & Compliance (UK Equality Act 2010)
- **Do NOT include**: Photos, date of birth, age, marital status, gender, nationality, or National Insurance numbers.
- **Contact Details**: City/Town and Postcode district (e.g. "London, EC2A"), UK phone number, email, LinkedIn, and GitHub. Full street addresses are unnecessary.

## Page Length & Terminology
- **Document Type**: In the UK, this document is formally referred to as a **Curriculum Vitae (CV)**.
- **Target Length**: Standard **2 pages** (A4 format). 1 page is acceptable only for fresh graduates.
- **Spelling**: Use British English throughout (e.g. *optimised, categorised, behaviour, programme, modelled*).

## Section Conventions
- **Personal Statement**: Begins with a tailored 3–4 sentence Personal Statement (instead of "Summary") outlining career focus, technical identity, and what you bring to the organisation.
- **Education**: Degree classifications are standard in the UK (e.g., First-Class Honours, Upper Second-Class Honours [2:1]). Include them if applicable.
- **References**: Mentioning "References available upon request" is traditional in the UK, but can be omitted if space is tight.
