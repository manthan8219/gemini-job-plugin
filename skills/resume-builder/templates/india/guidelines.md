# India Tech Resume Guidelines

## Layout & Format
- **Document Style**: Skills-forward, tech-dense, high-impact resume (1–2 pages depending on experience).
- **Format**: Standard A4 page layout, cleanly separated sections.

## Sections & Prominence
- **Technical Skills at Top**: Tech recruiters and hiring managers in India strongly prefer seeing the categorized skill matrix near the top of the resume immediately below the summary.
- **Scale & Volume Metrics**: Indian tech startups and product MNCs heavily prioritize scale indicators:
  - Daily Active Users (DAU), transactions per second (TPS), queries per second (QPS), and data volume (GB/TB).
  - Include exact metrics in experience bullet points.
- **Key Projects & Open Source**: A dedicated section for independent systems, high-scale hobby projects, or open-source contributions.

## Personal Information
- Name, Phone (+91 ...), Email, City (e.g. Bengaluru, Pune, Gurugram), LinkedIn, and GitHub.
- **Do NOT include**: Father's name, marital status, religion, caste, or date of birth (modern tech firms follow standard bias-free practices).
