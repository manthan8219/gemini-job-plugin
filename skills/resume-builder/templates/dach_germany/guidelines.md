# Germany, Austria & Switzerland (DACH) Lebenslauf Guidelines

## Format & Structure (Tabular & Factual)
- **Document Style**: The German *Lebenslauf* is traditionally structured, orderly, and strictly chronological or reverse-chronological without gaps (*lückenlos*).
- **Page Format**: Standard A4 format, 1–2 pages.
- **Tone**: Factual, precise, and objective. Avoid hyperbole or promotional buzzwords; focus on exact responsibilities, project scopes, and technical roles.

## Personal Information (Persönliche Daten)
- Include: Full Name, Current City/Country, Contact details (Phone, Email, LinkedIn, GitHub).
- **Language Proficiencies**: Standard CEFR scale rating is strictly expected in DACH (e.g., German: Fluent / C1; English: Professional / C2; French: Basic / A2).

## Technical Details
- In the German tech market, explicit listing of the technologies used *per role or per project* (e.g. *Tech Stack: Go, PostgreSQL, Kafka, Kubernetes*) is strongly favoured by tech leads.
