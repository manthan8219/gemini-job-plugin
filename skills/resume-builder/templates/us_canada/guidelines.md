# US & Canada Resume Guidelines

## Legal & Compliance (Strict Anti-Discrimination)
- **NEVER include**: Photos, headshots, date of birth, age, gender, marital status, race, religion, or nationality.
- **Contact Details**: City and State/Province only (e.g. "San Francisco, CA" or "Toronto, ON"). Avoid full street addresses. Include phone number, professional email, LinkedIn, and GitHub/Portfolio.

## Page Length & Layout
- **Target Length**: Strictly **1 page** for candidates with under 7–10 years of experience. Maximum 2 pages for principal/executive leaders.
- **Page Size**: US Letter format (8.5 x 11 inches).
- **Margins**: Clean 0.5" to 0.75" margins with no text boxes, side-by-side columns, or graphics that break ATS parsers.

## Phrasing & Formula
- Every bullet point must use Google's **XYZ Formula**:
  > *"Accomplished **[X]** as measured by **[Y]**, by doing **[Z]**"*
- Start every bullet with a strong past-tense action verb (e.g. *Architected, Engineered, Spearheaded, Reduced, Accelerated*).
- Focus heavily on business impact: revenue enabled, cost saved, latency reduced, scale handled.
