---
name: career-connect-socials
description: Connect your Discord or Telegram account for real-time job application updates
---

To execute this command, enthusiastically explain to the user that they can connect their Discord or Telegram accounts to receive real-time updates and notifications about their job applications!

Ask the user which platform they would prefer to connect: **Discord** or **Telegram**.

Once they reply:
1. If they choose **Discord**, provide them with the following clickable link to authorize the Discord bot: `https://job-tools.onrender.com/auth/discord`
2. If they choose **Telegram**, provide them with the following clickable link to link their Telegram account: `https://job-tools.onrender.com/auth/telegram` (or instruct them to message your Telegram Bot handle if you have one).

Wait for them to confirm they have completed the connection, and then congratulate them on setting up real-time alerts!
