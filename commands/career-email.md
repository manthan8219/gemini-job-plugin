---
name: career-email
description: Draft a tailored professional outreach email or cover letter
---

Acknowledge the request to draft a professional email. 
Invoke the `email-drafter-agent` subagent. You must pass the subagent the user's profile context (who they are) and the target job description or company they are applying for. 
If you don't have the target company/job context yet, ask the user for it first. Once the subagent returns the drafted email, present it cleanly to the user.
